# What changed between b10.1 and b11, and why — one page for the PR

Audience: whoever merges `adrc-b11` into the PR branch. The PR head (`adrc-toggle`, 6317fe2a, 4 Aug) equals the
tester tag `adrc-pr15400-b10.1`. b11 = that + 12 ADRC commits + upstream master 5d3e7c85 (19 Sep) merged in. The
ADRC-side diff is 11 files, +541 / −124 lines; the rest of the 147-file diff is upstream's own six weeks.

Branch `adrc-b11`, tag `adrc-pr15400-b11`, release with 629 hex files:
https://github.com/danusha2345/ADRC-betaflight/releases/tag/adrc-pr15400-b11

## 1. Behaviour changes, in the order a pilot meets them

| # | what | setting (default in b11) | why | evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Low `wc` while the liftoff gate is closed**, ramped to flight `wc` after liftoff | `adrc_ground_wc` 10, `adrc_wc_ramp_ms` 100 — **on** | The one failure that damaged a craft: with airmode the ground loop closes through the airframe and the mixer, its gain is `2·wc·wo/b0`, and a whoop lifts itself on arm. Lowering `wc` on the ground removes it; the ramp keeps the transition unfelt. | Air65 arm-time lift gone at 99/110 (jmsweng); clean arms on Petrel75, HDZ, Air65, Pavo20, TH3/2.5"; 5" tap test at 10 and 40, no fly-away, no bounce (jmsweng, 19 Sep). Guide §4, addenda 3–8. |
| 2 | **Cap on the ground `wc`** so item 1 is bounded per tune | `adrc_ground_dgain` 40 (= 4.0) | `dgain · b0 / (2·wo)` caps the ground D-path gain; at 4.0 the cap does not bind on any tune flown, so every craft gets ground wc 10. (11 Sep proposal was 40 / 1.0, where the cap binds at 14–24 rad/s on the whoops; both arm clean.) | Tap tests ordered by `2·wc·wo/b0` on two tunes (addendum 6). |
| 3 | **b0 schedule shape** | `adrc_b0_law` SQRT (was QUADRATIC) | QUADRATIC was the worst of the four on throttle steps: 122–160 °/s peak error vs 42–46 SQRT at the same hover. FIXED has the lowest knee but is the wrong default for a b0 fitted once at hover. | Addenda 2, 9; guide §3. |
| 4 | **b0 schedule may go below 1 under hover** | `adrc_b0_scale_min` 100 — **off** (opt-in 20–99) | Below hover the plant gain is lower than at hover, so a b0 pinned at the hover value under-gains the loop; this is what testers were curing with `thrust_linear` or `adrc_hover_throttle` 5. Testers settled on 70–90. | Addenda 9–11; exp5 bug (20 acted as off under `-ffast-math`) fixed in exp6. |
| 5 | **z3 growth inhibit while the mixer is pinned** | `adrc_sat_z3_inhibit` OFF — opt-in | "Yaw washout": with a motor on the floor and one on the ceiling the observer books the undelivered moment as disturbance on all three axes and z3 hits the `pidsum_limit` bound in 100–180 ms, then a 500–1100 °/s excursion. Reuses the gate's existing "z3 only toward zero" inhibit while the mixer clipped on the previous loop; `u` is not scaled. | 5 airframes, 4 OFF/ON pairs: I terms ≤ 333 vs 1000, a 0.37 s pin held within 48 °/s, no-op where the mixer never pins; full throttle and 780 °/s yaw spins do not pin. Addenda 12–21, tracker ADRC-033. |
| 6 | **Per-axis damping ratio** | `adrc_zeta_roll/pitch/yaw` 100 — opt-in | `D = 2·ζ·wc·z2/b0`; lets yaw `wc` rise without yaw D. **No benefit shown in any comparison**; candidate for *not* carrying into the PR line. | Addenda 13, 17, 19. |
| 7 | **PID-profile parameter group version 13 → 14** | — | The four profiles are one PG array restored by one memcpy; growing `pidProfile_t` without a bump mis-strides profiles 2–4 (found by adversarial review, ADRC-034). b11 therefore **resets the PID profiles on upgrade**; the exp2…8 notes that claimed otherwise carry an erratum. | Tracker ADRC-034; test `PidProfilesPgVersionRejectsOlderBlobs`. |

Unchanged: the control law, the ESO, the gate, `DEBUG_ADRC` = 102 (upstream's new modes were appended after it),
classic PID path untouched. The eight `DEBUG_SET(DEBUG_ADRC, …)` calls carry upstream's new `//!<` annotations.

## 2. What the merge did

`git merge upstream/master` into the tester line (no rebase; the PR's own history is kept). Conflicts: `src/test/Makefile`
(both test blocks kept) and `mixer.c` motor-stop condition (ADRC publish block kept on upstream's version incl.
`LAUNCH_MODE`). Upstream deltas in `mixer.c` verified line-identical by the review. New upstream tests required the
debug annotations and an `autopilotThrottleValid()` stub in `adrc_mixer_unittest`. 78 unit-test suites pass; G474 and
F722 build; NEXUSXR (F722) overflows ITCM on pristine master and is skipped by the release workflow.

## 3. What the review said (Codex, gpt-6-astra, adversarial, before release)

NO-GO on the first cut for two confirmed findings, both fixed in 61d2d881: the PG-array stride (item 7) and the
ADRC-033 predicate missing `MIXER_DYNAMIC` clips (`adrcMixWillClip()` added; four mixer tests now assert the flag,
mutation-checked). Confirmed as claimed: merge correctness, debug ordinal, annotations, defaults after reset. Left open
and agreed: the inhibit is global across axes and unbounded in time; default stays OFF until a sustained-clip case is
flown (jmsweng: weight under a motor, planned).
